# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nubs: Island Pets & Widgets ("Nubs," the "App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Nubs iOS application.

Nubs is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not require an account, does not operate a backend server, and does not send your game data to us.

## 1. Summary

Nubs does not collect personal data from users.

In particular:

* We do not require accounts or logins.
* We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information through the App.
* We do not collect location information.
* We do not collect photos, videos, contacts, messages, or other user content.
* We do not collect advertising identifiers.
* We do not track users across apps or websites.
* We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
* We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.
* We do not sell personal information.
* We do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.
* We do not operate a backend server that receives your game data.

Your Nubs game information stays on your device.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the App work, Nubs stores certain information locally on your iPhone.

This may include:

* Your selected Nubs and characters
* Unlocked characters
* Virtual coin balances
* Virtual items
* Character packs and purchase status
* Streaks
* Pet care and interaction progress
* Game progress
* App preferences and settings
* Island Session state
* Widget information
* Other information needed to maintain your local game experience

This information is stored locally within the App's storage and, where necessary for Home Screen widgets or related iOS features, within the App's local App Group container.

This information is not uploaded to Nubs or to a Nubs-operated server.

## 3. Widgets, Dynamic Island, Lock Screen, and Live Activities

Nubs can display your characters through features provided by iOS, including:

* Home Screen widgets
* Lock Screen features
* Dynamic Island
* Live Activities

When you start an Island Session or use a widget, the App may make locally stored game information available to the Nubs widget or Live Activity components on your device so that your selected character and related information can be displayed.

This information is used locally for App functionality and is not sent to us.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Nubs may offer in-app purchases, including purchases of:

* Virtual coins
* Premium characters
* Character packs
* Other digital items or premium features

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your:

* Credit card number
* Debit card number
* Apple Pay payment information
* Billing credentials
* Full payment details

Apple handles payment processing under Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

The App may receive information from StoreKit on your device that is necessary to determine whether a purchase has been completed or is eligible to be restored. Purchase entitlements may also be restored using Apple's purchase restoration mechanisms.

We do not operate a server that stores your payment information or purchase history.

## 5. Notifications

Nubs may use local notifications to remind you about your Nubs, streaks, pet care, or other App features.

If notifications are enabled, they are scheduled and handled on your device using Apple's notification system.

Nubs does not operate a push-notification server and does not collect notification-related personal information.

You can control notification permissions at any time through your iPhone's Settings app.

## 6. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Nubs does not currently include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

We do not use the App to:

* Build advertising profiles
* Track you across other companies' apps or websites
* Deliver targeted advertising
* Measure advertising behavior across third-party services
* Sell or rent user information

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and our App Store privacy disclosures as required before or when those practices are introduced.

## 7. Children and Family Privacy

Nubs is designed to be family-friendly and may be used by children.

We take children's privacy seriously.

Nubs does not knowingly collect personal information from children, including children under 13. In fact, the App is designed so that we do not collect personal information from users of any age.

Children do not need to provide a:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Location
* Photo
* Account
* Username
* Profile
* Message
* Other personal information

to play Nubs.

Because no user account or backend database exists, we do not maintain profiles or personal records about children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information has somehow been provided to us through the App, you may contact us at info@nubsofficial.com.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data is stored locally on your device for as long as the App remains installed, subject to normal iOS storage behavior.

Because we do not receive or maintain this game data on our servers, we do not have a copy of it and cannot access, recover, export, or remotely delete it for you.

Deleting Nubs from your device will delete the App's locally stored game data and associated local App Group data, subject to Apple's operating-system behavior.

Deleting the App does not necessarily delete records maintained separately by Apple relating to App Store transactions. Apple may retain purchase records and may allow eligible purchases to be restored through your Apple account.

## 9. Data Security

Nubs is designed to minimize privacy risk by keeping game data on your device instead of maintaining a central user database.

Your locally stored information is also subject to the security protections provided by your device and iOS.

No method of storing information can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, but reducing unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information is a core part of the App's design.

## 10. App Store Privacy Disclosure

Based on the App's current design, we represent Nubs in App Store Connect as an app for which we do not collect data from the App.

If Nubs later introduces features that transmit or collect user data, analytics, advertising technology, accounts, cloud synchronization, or other data practices, we will update our App Store privacy disclosures and this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

## 11. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple technologies and services necessary to distribute and operate an iOS application, including services such as the App Store, StoreKit, widgets, Live Activities, and other iOS frameworks.

Apple's handling of information through its own services is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Nubs does not control information that Apple independently collects or processes as part of your Apple account, App Store activity, device operation, or payment processing.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Because we do not operate user accounts or maintain a database of personal information collected through Nubs, we generally do not have personal information from the App to access, correct, sell, share, or delete.

If you have a question about whether we possess information relating to you, you may contact us at info@nubsofficial.com.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits any rights you may have under applicable privacy law.
